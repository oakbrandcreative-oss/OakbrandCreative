Yours to keep

What you own.

  • Your domain and logo files are transferred to you when the project is complete. They are yours, whether or not you keep the care plan. After the first year, you pay the yearly domain renewal (about $10 to $20) to the registrar.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How fast can I be live?

Website and Essential: 3 business days. Business: 7. Pro: 10. The clock starts when I have everything I need from you, and it pauses while I wait for your feedback or approvals. So the faster you reply, the faster you launch. Need it sooner? Ask about a 24-hour rush when you apply (Website and Essential only).

What is the care plan?

It's the monthly fee that keeps your website online and your email working. It covers hosting and your small changes each month.

What if I cancel?

After the 12-month minimum, you can cancel with 30 days' notice. Your website then goes offline at the end of the notice period, and you keep your domain, logo and design files. Email hosting ends when you cancel, so you choose your own provider, and I help with the handover. Payment and refund terms are in the agreement I send with your payment link.

What counts as a small change?

Text, photos and small updates. Unused small changes don't roll over. A redesign or new pages are quoted separately.

What counts as a feature?

The usual simple website extras, such as a contact form or a map. Ecommerce isn't included.

What's the difference between Essential and Business?

Essential includes a logo designed by me, with changes to colours and fonts. Business and Pro include custom logo design, with 3 designs to choose from, and a more developed brand.

Can I bring my own logo?

Yes. That's the Website package. Send your logo as a PNG and I build around it.

I don't know what style I want.

For Business and Pro, the 3 logo designs to choose from are a good place to start. You pick the one that feels right, then use your included rounds of changes. If you're still not happy, talk to me. I'd rather get it right than leave you with something you don't love.

Why do we talk before I pay?

Every project is different. A short conversation first makes sure the package fits and that you know exactly what you get before any money changes hands.

How do I pay?

After we've talked through your project and you've sent your details, I email you a payment link. Website and Essential are paid in full. Business and Pro start with 50%, and the other 50% is due when the work is complete. Business and Pro clients then book a call with me.

How does business email work?

One mailbox on your domain is included in every care plan. Extra mailboxes are $4 a month each. I set it up and send you the login.

What if I need something different?

Tell me what you need and I'll send you a quote.
